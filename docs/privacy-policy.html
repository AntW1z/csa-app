Privacy Policy — Georgia Tech CSA App

Last updated: July 28, 2026

This app is operated by the Georgia Tech Chinese Student Association ("CSA," "we," "us") for use by our members and the broader Georgia Tech community. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and your choices.

Information We Collect

Account information. If you create an account, we collect your email address and a display name (which you choose and can change at any time).

Membership information. If you request club membership, we store your membership status (pending/member), the term you were approved for (semester or full year), and your role within the club (member, moderator, or admin, for club officers).

Push notification token. If you allow notifications, we store a device token (provided by Apple/Google's push notification services) used to send you club announcements and event reminders. This token doesn't identify you personally on its own.

App activity. We store which in-app notifications you've read or dismissed, so that state stays consistent if you use the app on multiple devices.

Content you create. If you're a club officer (moderator/admin), any posts, event listings, sponsor listings, or images you create through the app are stored and may be publicly visible to other users, since that's the purpose of that content.

Photos. If you're a club officer uploading an image (for an event, sponsor, or the home page), that image is uploaded to our cloud storage provider. We don't access your photo library beyond the specific image you choose to upload.

What We Don't Collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, or browsing activity outside the app. We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

How We Use Your Information

  • To operate your account and verify membership status
  • To send you club announcements and event reminders, if you've enabled notifications
  • To let club officers manage membership and post club content
  • To maintain a record of moderator actions (e.g. who approved a membership request) for accountability within the club's leadership

Who Can See Your Information

Basic profile information (display name, email, role, membership status) is visible to other signed-in users of the app, and to club officers who manage membership. Members-only content is intended to be visible only to approved members, though this is enforced by the app rather than the underlying database.

Third-Party Services

The app is built on Firebase (a Google Cloud service), which provides our account system, database, file storage, and handles push notification delivery in partnership with Apple and Google's own push notification services. These providers process data on our behalf and maintain their own security and privacy practices. We do not control, and are not responsible for, the privacy practices of these providers beyond our use of them.

Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from within the app (Profile → Delete account), which permanently removes your account and profile information. This cannot be undone. Content created by club officers as part of official club records (e.g. past event listings) may be retained as club history even after that individual's account is removed.

Children's Privacy

This app is intended for college students and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
