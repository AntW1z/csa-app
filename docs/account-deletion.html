Delete Your Account — Georgia Tech CSA App

Last updated: August 22, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Georgia Tech CSA ("CSA") app account and what happens to your data when you do.

How to Delete Your Account

The fastest way is directly in the app:

  1. Open the app and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm the deletion. If you signed in a while ago, you may be asked to re-enter your password first — this is a security step required before permanently deleting an account.

If you no longer have the app installed or can't access your account, email us at ferostudio.dev@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account and we'll process the deletion for you.

What Gets Deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Your profile information (display name, class year, membership status)
  • Your device's push notification registration
  • Your read/dismissed status for in-app notifications

This action cannot be undone.

What's Retained

Some information is kept as part of the club's ongoing records rather than being deleted along with your account:

  • Content created by club officers as part of official club records (e.g. past event listings you may have interacted with)
  • Your name on any event check-in records you submitted
  • Any feedback or bug reports you submitted

If you'd like any of this retained information removed as well, contact us at ferostudio.dev@gmail.com and we'll handle it directly — there's just no dedicated in-app control for it yet.
