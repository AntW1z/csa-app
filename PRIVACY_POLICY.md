# Privacy Policy — Georgia Tech CSA App

*Last updated: August 18, 2026*

This app is operated by the Georgia Tech Chinese Student Association
("CSA," "we," "us") for use by our members and the broader Georgia Tech
community. This policy explains what information the app collects, how
it's used, and your choices.

## Information We Collect

**Account information.** If you create an account, we collect your email
address and a display name (which you choose and can change at any time).

**Class year.** We ask which year you're in (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, or
4th+/graduate) — either right after you create an account, or the first
time you check in to an event if you made your account before we started
asking. This is used for event planning and attendance statistics (see
"Event check-in" below); it's optional in the sense that you can decline
to provide it and simply not use event check-in.

**Membership information.** If you request club membership, we store your
membership status (pending/member), the term you were approved for
(semester or full year), and your role within the club (member, moderator,
or admin, for club officers).

**Event check-in.** If an event has check-in enabled, checking in stores
your name, class year, and a timestamp against that event's record. Club
officers use this to see headcount and class-year turnout per event, both
individually and in aggregate. This creates a record of which events
you've personally checked in to.

**Feedback you submit.** If you submit feedback or a bug report through
the app, we store the message along with your name and email so club
officers can respond or follow up if needed.

**Push notification token.** If you allow notifications, we store a device
token (provided by Apple/Google's push notification services) used to
send you club announcements and event reminders. This token doesn't
identify you personally on its own.

**App activity.** We store which in-app notifications you've read or
dismissed, so that state stays consistent if you use the app on multiple
devices.

**Content you create.** If you're a club officer (moderator/admin), any
posts, event listings, sponsor listings, or images you create through the
app are stored and may be publicly visible to other users, since that's
the purpose of that content.

**Photos.** If you're a club officer uploading an image (for an event,
sponsor, or the home page), that image is uploaded to our cloud storage
provider. We don't access your photo library beyond the specific image you
choose to upload.

## What We Don't Collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, or browsing activity outside
the app. We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not sell
or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

## How We Use Your Information

- To operate your account and verify membership status
- To send you club announcements and event reminders, if you've enabled
  notifications
- To let club officers manage membership and post club content
- To maintain a record of moderator actions (e.g. who approved a
  membership request) for accountability within the club's leadership
- To track event attendance and generate participation statistics
  (headcount and class-year breakdown) for club officers' event planning
- To follow up on feedback or bug reports you submit

## Who Can See Your Information

Basic profile information (display name, email, role, membership status,
class year) is visible to other signed-in users of the app, and to club
officers who manage membership. Members-only content is intended to be
visible only to approved members, though this is enforced by the app
rather than the underlying database.

Event check-in records (your name, class year, and check-in time) are
stored on the event itself; club officers can view individual attendee
records and aggregate statistics through the club's management tools.
Feedback you submit is visible only to club officers.

## Third-Party Services

The app is built on **Firebase** (a Google Cloud service), which provides
our account system, database, file storage, and handles push notification
delivery in partnership with Apple and Google's own push notification
services. These providers process data on our behalf and maintain their
own security and privacy practices. We do not control, and are not
responsible for, the privacy practices of these providers beyond our use
of them.

## Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from within the app (**Profile →
Delete account**), which permanently removes your account and profile
information. This cannot be undone. Content created by club officers as
part of official club records (e.g. past event listings) may be retained
as club history even after that individual's account is removed — this
also includes your name on any event check-in records and any feedback
you've submitted, which remain as part of those records rather than being
deleted along with your account. Contact us if you'd like these removed
separately.

## Children's Privacy

This app is intended for college students and is not directed at children
under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app
after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
